We need to refactor our price fetching and display even more.

To get SOL prices in fiat equivalent, we use silkyway/src/app/api/prices/sol-usd/route.ts, which fetches real-time SOL-USD prices every 30 seconds.

We then use :

- silkyway/src/app/api/prices/usd-eur/route.ts : CONVERTS USD TO EUR
- silkyway/src/app/api/prices/usd-gbp/route.ts : CONVERTS USD TO GBP

Here's what we want :

In silkyway/src/app/(routes)/create/page.tsx and EditListingForm, we have the logic that allows users to create/update a listing in their preffered fiat currency, which they can set with CurrencySelector.
Basically, when they create a listing, users set a price in fiat currency (USD, EUR or GBP). 
These pages will also display the converted equivalent in SOL (which basis is SOL-USD, using silkyway/src/app/api/prices/sol-usd/route.ts).

Then, the listing is created/updated and recorded in the database with the fiat currency used during creation.

Example :

Let's say that, while having EUR as my prefered currency in CurrencySelector, I create a listing with a price of 100 EUR.

Now, if I visit this listing while having EUR as my prefered currency in CurrencySelector, the listing price in ListingClient, ListingCard and SmallListingCard should be displayed as :

100 €
Converted equivalent in SOL

Now, if I switch the CurrencySelector to USD, the listing price in ListingClient, ListingCard and SmallListingCard will be displayed as :

Converted equivalent of 100 € in USD
Converted equivalent in SOL

If I switch the CurrencySelector to GBP, the listing price in ListingClient, ListingCard and SmallListingCard will be displayed as :

Converted equivalent of 100 € in GBP
Converted equivalent in SOL
